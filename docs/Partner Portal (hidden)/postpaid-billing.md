The Postpaid billing option is available for select partners.

You will receive invoices from Gupshup based on your monthly usage on partner portal. You no longer need to recharge your wallet to use Gupshup services. Postpaid billing is hassle free option which is available to select customers. 

> 📘 In order to move to postpaid billing, please raise a request [partner.support@gupshup.io](mailto:partner.support@gupshup.io)

There will not be any commissions applicable for postpaid customers. You can view your customers consumption across Gupshup apps under the **Analytics** tab on Partner Portal.
