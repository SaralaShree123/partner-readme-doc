# Business Profiles

A **business profile** is the public WhatsApp profile for a business phone number — display name, about text, address, email, websites, profile photo, and related details.

## Why it matters

- Users decide whether to trust and message the business from profile info
- Display name approval and branding consistency start here
- Partners can read/update profiles via Partner Portal or [Business Profile APIs](/docs/business-profile)
