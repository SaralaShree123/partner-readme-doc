# Quality rating

The **phone number quality rating** reflects how recipients respond to messages from a WhatsApp Business number over roughly the previous seven days. It is driven by customer feedback signals such as blocks and reports.

## Where to find it

**Meta Business Manager → WhatsApp Manager → Phone numbers** — see the Quality rating column.

## States

- High (green)
- Medium (yellow)
- Low (red)

High quality supports eligibility for better messaging capacity and features. On yellow/red, check WhatsApp Manager insights (including block feedback when Meta provides it).

## Block reason signals

When users block a business they may select reasons such as spam, offensive messages, didn’t sign up, no longer needed, or no reason. Use these signals to improve templates and opt-in practices.

## Historical view

In Phone numbers settings you can review quality over recent days to spot trends.
