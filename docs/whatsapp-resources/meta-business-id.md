# Meta Business ID

The **Meta Business ID** uniquely identifies a Meta Business Portfolio. Support teams may ask for it to locate the correct portfolio.

It may also be called Business Portfolio ID, Meta Business Manager ID, FBM ID, or Business Manager ID.

## Find your Business ID

1. Open Meta Business Suite.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Open **Business Info** — the Business portfolio ID is shown at the top.

## FAQ

**How do I know which portfolio owns a WABA?**  
In WhatsApp Manager, check the portfolio name for that WABA. The ID under the WABA name is the **WABA ID**, not the Business ID.

**Can I change the Meta Business ID?**  
No. Meta Business IDs cannot be edited.
