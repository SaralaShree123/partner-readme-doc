# Display Names

A **display name** is the business name shown to users in WhatsApp chats and notifications. It must relate to the business and follow WhatsApp Commerce and Business policies.

## When users see the display name

Users may see the phone number instead of the display name until:

- The display name status is **approved** in Meta Business Suite
- The business has completed **Meta Business Verification**
- The account has active messaging history and maintains high quality

For coexistence (COEX) numbers, display name review may require Meta Verified first.

## Meta principles (summary)

- Must not violate WhatsApp policies
- Accurately represents the business, product, department, or clearly labeled test/demo account
- Matches external branding (website capitalization/spacing)
- Clear relationship to the business on the website
- Requires a working verified website

Before registration you can change the display name freely; after registration, Meta typically requires waiting **30 days** between change requests.

## Changing a display name

Update the display name in **WhatsApp Manager** (business profile) or via Partner APIs / Partner Portal where available. See [Business Profiles](/docs/business-profiles-overview).
