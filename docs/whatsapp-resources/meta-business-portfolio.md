# Meta Business Portfolio

A **Meta business portfolio** (formerly Meta Business Manager) lets organisations manage Meta business assets in one place. On the WhatsApp Business Platform, a portfolio is mainly the container for WhatsApp Business Accounts (WABAs).

You must have a business portfolio to use the WhatsApp Business Platform.

## Key points

- A company can have multiple portfolios; keep one unless there is a clear reason to separate assets.
- A portfolio can contain multiple WABAs.
- Each portfolio can register a limited number of phone numbers (often up to 20 as limits increase).

## Create a business portfolio

### Before you begin

- Access Meta Business Suite with a Facebook profile or managed Meta account.
- Check whether a portfolio already exists for the organisation to avoid duplicates.

### In Meta Business Suite

1. Go to [business.facebook.com](https://business.facebook.com/) and sign in.
2. Open the drop-down under **Home**.
3. Choose **Create a business portfolio**.
4. Enter a public business name (no special characters).
5. Confirm your details / email if prompted, then create the portfolio.

You can also create a portfolio during [Embedded Signup](/docs/what-is-embedded-signup).
