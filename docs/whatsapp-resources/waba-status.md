# WABA Status

A WABA can move through statuses that affect whether numbers can message. Partners should monitor status in Meta WhatsApp Manager and via Partner health APIs where available.

## Common ideas

- **Approved / connected** — normal messaging (subject to limits and quality)
- **Pending / review** — Meta is reviewing business info, website, or naming; messaging may be limited
- **Restricted / banned** — policy or integrity issues; messaging blocked until resolved
- **Rejected** — onboarding or naming failed requirements

Exact labels vary in Meta UI. Always check **Account Quality** / WhatsApp Manager for the current state and required actions.
