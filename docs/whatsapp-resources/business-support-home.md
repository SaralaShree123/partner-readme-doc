# Business Support Home

**Meta Business Support Home** helps businesses monitor and resolve policy, advertising, and account-quality issues that can affect WABAs and related assets.

Use it when:

- A WABA or ad account has restrictions
- Feature access is limited after creating a new portfolio
- Meta asks for additional verification or appeals

Open: [Business Support Home](https://www.facebook.com/business-support-home/)

For Gupshup platform issues (APIs, wallet, Partner Portal), contact [Partner Support](/docs/support).
