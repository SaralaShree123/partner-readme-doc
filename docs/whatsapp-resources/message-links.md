# Message Links

**Message links** (click-to-chat links) open WhatsApp with a prefilled conversation to a business phone number. They are commonly used on websites, ads, QR codes, and email.

## Typical format

Links usually follow Meta’s `wa.me` / WhatsApp click-to-chat patterns with the full international phone number (no `+` in some formats) and an optional prefilled text parameter.

## Partner tips

- Use the registered WhatsApp Business number, not a personal number
- Keep prefilled text compliant and useful
- Combine with [Ads that Click to WhatsApp](https://www.facebook.com/business/help) style entry points where relevant
