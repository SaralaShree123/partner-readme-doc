# Official Business Account (OBA)

An **Official Business Account (OBA)** — often called the blue badge / blue checkmark — helps users recognize notable, authentic brands on WhatsApp.

## Why it matters

- Increases trust in chat threads
- Usually requires strong brand presence, compliant display name, and Meta Business Verification
- Not all businesses qualify; Meta controls eligibility

## Related requirements

- Approved [display name](/docs/display-names)
- [Meta Business Verification](/docs/meta-business-verification)
- Healthy [quality rating](/docs/quality-rating)

Coexistence numbers may have different OBA / badge behaviour — confirm current Meta rules for COEX.
