# Two-factor authentication (2FA)

WhatsApp Business phone numbers use **two-step verification (2FA)** — a PIN that protects the number from being registered elsewhere without authorization.

## Why partners care

- Registration, migration, and some support flows may require the current 2FA PIN
- Losing the PIN can block number moves until Meta / BSP support processes are followed
- Disabling or resetting 2FA must be done carefully to avoid takeover risk

## Practical guidance

- Store the PIN securely when onboarding a customer number
- Only disable 2FA when required for a controlled migration or recovery process
- Prefer completing changes through documented Partner / Meta flows
