# Meta-provided (555) phone numbers

In some Embedded Signup paths, Meta may offer a **Meta-provided phone number** (often described as a +1 555-style number) instead of registering a business-owned number.

## When it appears

During Embedded Signup, a business may choose:

- **Add a new number** (recommended for production) — register a business-owned number
- **Use a display name only / Meta-provided number** — only when Meta makes the option available and eligibility rules are met

## Guidance for partners

- Prefer a real business-owned number for long-term production use
- Meta-provided numbers can be useful for limited testing scenarios when offered
- Eligibility and availability are controlled by Meta and may change
