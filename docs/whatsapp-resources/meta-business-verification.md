# Meta Business Verification

**Meta Business Verification** confirms that a Meta Business Portfolio represents a real organisation. Verification unlocks or improves WhatsApp capabilities such as display name visibility, higher throughput eligibility, and Official Business Account pathways.

## Why partners care

- Improves trust signals for customer WABAs
- Often required before display names show to end users
- Supports scaling paths for [messaging limits](/docs/messaging-limits)

## Types (high level)

- **Standard / Classic Business Verification** — Meta’s official verification across Business Suite assets
- **Partner-led Business Verification (PLBV)** — available in some partner programs to speed verification for eligible businesses

Exact eligibility depends on Meta program rules and region.
