# Introduction

This guide outlines the key changes and enhancements to the Wallet PMP Impact, Partner API changes to help users adapt smoothly, introduced Per-Message Pricing (PMP) in alignment with Meta's new pricing model for the WhatsApp Business Platform, effective July 1, 2025.

# Wallet Enhancements

## Wallet Home Page

Below are the New Cards added in the UI:

* Marketing Templates
* Authentication Templates
* Utility Templates
* International Authentication Templates

## Wallet Usage Page

Introduced a pie chart and that displays the breakdown of templates used with the following categories as:

* Marketing Templates
* Utility Templates
* Authentication Templates
* International Authentication Templates
* Free Messages (Free tier, Free Utility, Referrals)

## Wallet Statement

Wallet Statement now displays the following Categories in WhatsApp Fee (waFees):

* Marketing Templates
* Marketing Templates (MM Lite)
* Authentication Templates
* Utility Templates
* International Authentication Templates
