# Introduction

## Purpose of the Guide

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for using the **Partner Customer Portal**, enabling partner customers to manage their **WhatsApp Business Accounts (WABAs)** and their associated message templates.

The portal delivers a white-labeled experience for seamless WhatsApp Business API management for businesses.

## Target Audience

This guide is intended for partner customers who need to configure and manage their WABAs, create and update message templates, and monitor their WhatsApp communication.

## Overview

The Partner Customer Portal is a user-friendly interface designed to allow businesses to efficiently manage their WhatsApp Business Accounts. The portal provides functionalities for:

* **WABA Management** – Add, configure, and maintain WhatsApp Business Accounts.
* **Template Management** – Create, submit, and track approval statuses for message templates.
* **Monitoring & Reporting** – View usage metrics and logs for WhatsApp messaging activities.

## Walkthrough

### Managing Channels

1. Navigate to the **Channels** tab on the left hand side navigation.
2. Click Whatsapp under channels to open up Whatsapp management section.

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/a7f7de93367667130b1d0c65adb8066d0930494615254aed0a8390524308d365-5..png" />

### Creating and Managing Message Templates

1. Go to the **Templates** section.

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/5e90524dadcef878631d36165bd653431c7b745716e8aa99336bc6a3ae57046f-6..png" />

2. Click **Create Template** and fill in details:
   1. **Category** (e.g., Transactional, Marketing, OTP)
   2. **Message Content** (including variables if needed)
   3. **Language** selection.

3. Submit the template for **Meta approval**.

4. Track the **status** of submitted templates:
   1. **Approved** – Ready for use.
   2. **Rejected** – Requires modification based on Meta’s feedback.

### Billing

1. Access the **Billing** section.

   <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/f3c61ae614b8f532d932d95777faf22dbeacd773591aafc2907a7ca580b32778-7..png" />

   1. In case of INR wallets complete the KYC for your wallet providing GSTIN number.
3. View your wallet balance, usage overview and detailed metrics for your Whatsapp usage
4. .You can read more about the wallet section [here](https://partner-docs.gupshup.io/docs/commissions).
