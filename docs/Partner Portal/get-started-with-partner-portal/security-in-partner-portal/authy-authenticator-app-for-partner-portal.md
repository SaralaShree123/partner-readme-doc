Using the Authy authenticator app for two-factor authentication (2FA) enhances the security of your Partner Portal account. This guide will walk you through the setup process step-by-step, including images to help visualize each stage.

# Download and Install Authy

* **Download the App**: Go to the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android) and search for "Authy". Download and install the app.
* **Open Authy**: Launch the app on your device.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/5991738d1e371e9044d8074dfa60713de8bc9d8e618ef60db6050f1596701c06-Authy.PNG" />

# Register Your Account

* **Enter Your Phone Number**: Input your phone number and tap "Next". You will receive a verification code via SMS or a phone call.
* **Verify Your Number**: Enter the verification code you received to complete your registration.

# Set Up Two-Factor Authentication for the Partner Portal

1. **Log into Partner Portal**: Access your Partner Portal ([https://partner.gupshup.io](https://partner.gupshup.io)) account and navigate to your profile settings. Click on your name in the top left corner, then select **Profile**.
2. **Activate 2FA**: Scroll down to find the option for Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and click on it. This will generate a QR code on your screen.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/6f3b3f02c54134eba58639aff254b7e5f4e74feba1ca5e1c9e74954ac160aca7-image-20241015-050006.png" />

# Pair Authy with Partner Portal

* **Open Authy**: In the Authy app, tap the "+" button or "Add Account".
* **Scan QR Code**: Select "Scan QR Code" and point your camera at the QR code displayed on your Partner Portal screen. Allow camera access if prompted.
* **Save Account**: Once scanned, Authy automatically recognizes the service name (Partner Portal). Tap **Save**.

# Complete 2FA Setup

Enter Code: Go back to Partner Portal and enter the six-digit code displayed in Authy. This code changes every 30 seconds, so make sure to enter it promptly.

# Successful Setup Confirmation

After entering the correct code, displays a confirmation message indicating that 2FA has been successfully set up.

<Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/d84a96bd61f5623f5f33088470a055db7f66b1c476d8237b0d51b75992bdc162-image.png" />

# Logging In with 2FA

The next time you log into the Partner Portal account, after entering your username and password, you will be prompted to enter a code from Authy.

* Open Authy, select your Partner Portal account, and input the current six-digit code to access your account securely.

# Important Notes

* Codes in Authy refresh every 30 seconds; ensure you enter them quickly.

By following these steps, you can effectively enhance the security of your Partner Portal account using the Authy Authenticator app.
