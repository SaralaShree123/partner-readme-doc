# Introduction

This guide outlines the automation process for wire transfers in partner wallets and provides a step-by-step breakdown of creating and managing a wire transfer request.

# What is a Wire Transfer?

Wire transfer is an electronic method of sending money from one bank account to another. It is secure, fast, and reliable for transferring funds between financial institutions, domestically and internationally.

# Who can use Wire Transfer?

Partners with access to the Gupshup partner portal who initiate wire transfers to add funds to their wallets. Wire transfers are currently supported in USD.

# How do you raise a Wire Transfer Request on the Gupshup Partner Portal?

To raise a wire transfer request, follow these steps:

Before you start, drop a request to enable wire transfers for your wallet via partner support [partner.support@gupshup.io](partner.support@gupshup.io) and your CSM.

1. **Log in to the Partner Portal**
   * Navigate to the **Wallet** tab.
2. **Initiate the Wire Transfer Request**

   * Click on the **Recharge** icon.  This page navigates to the Billing page.
   * Select the **Add Credits** button.
   * To enable the Payment Gateway, enter the required amount in the Recharge credits field.
   * Select the **Pay via Wire Transfer** button from the drop-down.

   * Enter the amount and bank details for the transfer.
   * Click **Proceed**.

   <Image align="center" width="50% " src="https://files.readme.io/dd1a4816f07c21096e4f04940b0ab78038162628ab3d306f64518a318b08c030-Billing-3.png" />

   * A proforma invoice is generated and ready to download.

   <Image align="center" width="50% " src="https://files.readme.io/265d7bd65710244a815877995a28fcc8b4d0df25477a31460d741befe7bc0daa-Billing-4.png" />
3. **Submit Payment Proof**
   * Partners must upload proof of payment along with the payment confirmation number.
   * Click on **Proceed**.
   * If payment proof is not uploaded within 5 days, the request is auto-rejected.
4. **Gupshup Team Verification**\
   Gupshup reviews the request and checks if the payment has been received in the company’s bank account.
   * The status of the request can be set to:
     * **Received**: Processing by finance.
     * **Credited**: Funds credited to the partner’s wallet.
     * **Rejected**: Payment not received.
5. **Confirmation**

   * Once the funds are credited, the Gupshup team uploads the final invoice in 2-4 business days.
   * The invoice is then available for the partner to download on the recharge history page.

# User Interfaces

## Partner Portal

* **Wire Transfer Button**: Allows partners to initiate a wire transfer.
* **Request History**: Partners can view the status of current and past transfers. The following statutes are available in the finance dashboard:
  * **Pending**: Awaiting proof or pending Gupshup review.
  * **Received**: Payment received, processing.
  * **Credited**: Funds credited to wallet.
  * **Invoiced**: Final invoice available for download.
  * **Rejected**: Payment not found, wire transfer rejected, or due to some other reason, the wire transfer has been rejected.

# Do's and Don’ts

## Do's:

* Ensure that you provide accurate bank details and payment amounts.
* Upload the payment confirmation proof within the specified time (within two days of the request).
* Track your wire transfer status on the portal regularly.

## Don’ts:

* Do not submit incorrect or incomplete information in the request form.
* Avoid delays in uploading the payment proof to prevent auto-rejection.

# How to Track the Wire Transfer Request Status

The user can track the status of the wire transfer request on the Billing > Recharge History page. Select the status from the drop-down list as one of the following:

* **Pending**: Awaiting payment proof submission.
* **Received**: Payment proof submitted, verification in progress.
* **Credited**: Funds have been credited to your wallet.
* **Invoiced**: The final invoice is available for download.
* **Rejected**: The request has been rejected (due to missing or invalid payment).
