## Introduction

The Tech Provider partners can now mark the app as a demo for personal use by using the Gupshup Partner Portal. This app allows the partner to onboard with their own Facebook Business Manager (FBM) without registering the joint solution ID.

## Steps to Mark an App as a Demo

1. Use either the self-serve portal or the Partner Portal.
2. Click on the Create App icon.

   <Image align="left" width="70% " src="https://files.readme.io/b788555-1.png" />
3. Ensure to link the app with your partner ID in case of self-serve.
4. Go to the apps screen on the Partner Portal and select the edit icon next to the app (only accessible by Partner Portal Admins).

   <Image align="left" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/671017a-3.png" />
5. Before using the app, toggle the demo mode switch to ON (default is off).

   <Image align="left" className="border" width="50% " border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/4dbef52-4.png" />
6. Ensure the go-live flow from the self-serve or Partner Portal Apps section.
7. Select your FBM tied to your Tech Provider.
