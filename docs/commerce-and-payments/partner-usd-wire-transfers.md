## What is a Wire Transfer?

Wire transfer is an electronic method of sending money from one bank account to another. It is secure, fast, and reliable for transferring funds between financial institutions, domestically and internationally.

## Step by Step Guide to Request on the Gupshup Partner Portal?

To raise a wire transfer request, follow these steps:

Before you start, drop a request to enable wire transfers for your wallet via partner support [partner.support@gupshup.io](mailto:partner.support@gupshup.io) and your CSM.

1. Log in to the Partner Portal

* Navigate to the Wallet tab.


2. Initiate the Wire Transfer Request

* Click on the Recharge icon. 
* This page navigates to the Billing page.
* Select the Add Credits button.
*
  To enable the Payment Gateway, enter the required amount in the Recharge credits field.
* Select the Pay via Wire Transfer button from the drop-down.

* Enter the amount and bank details for the transfer. The minimum amount to enter is $10000. 
* Click Proceed.
