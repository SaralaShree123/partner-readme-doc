# Partner Portal

* Login into the Partner Portal.
* Once logged in you will be at the Apps Dashboard.
* Select Create New App.

<Image align="center" className="border" width="75% " border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/bdd94503108a947183637a516e48cd12a9e8d54f3f2f2e4fe2eaf94e3b4fcda2-1.PNG" />

* Name the App according to your liking.

<Image align="center" className="border" width="75% " border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/9c8a19d03bc2215ebfee16238d6106348695572f56af5e570f93c045156c4f33-2.PNG" />

* Find your newly created App and select Go Live.

<Image align="center" className="border" width="75% " border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/6adcac56913941c3cd7141dacd7b1b9db96c0d3276de8b76a3f3abf64476cb3d-3.PNG" />

* Now fill in all the necessary data.

<Image align="center" className="border" width="75% " border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/18b5617193a26bcae3b982b2eb69448488694898120504c1d0ccb7284b09dca9-4.PNG" />

<Image align="center" className="border" width="75% " border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/af94424f1add592a253b87d97ba16de38238b620ae71a870387458ce5c919007-5.PNG" />

* In the 3rd step FB embedded flow will open, complete the flow, and your app will be live.

Now you can start sending messages.
