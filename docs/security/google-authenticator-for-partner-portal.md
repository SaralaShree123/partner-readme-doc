Google Authenticator is a widely used app for two-factor authentication (2FA), enhancing the security of user accounts by generating time-based one-time passcodes (TOTP). This guide will walk you through the steps to set up and use Google Authenticator specifically for accessing the Partner Portal.

# Step 1: Install Google Authenticator

## Download and Install the Google Authenticator App

* **Download the App**
  * For Android: Visit the Google Play Store and search for **Google Authenticator**.
  * For iOS: Go to the App Store and search for **Google Authenticator**.
  * Install the app on your mobile device.

<Image align="center" className="border" width="50% " border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/b6ddc09c601fd143572214e25b77904a2304ae93544a194936523a66cba34d0d-google-auth.png" />

## Step 2: Enable Two-Factor Authentication on Your Account

1. **Log into the Partner Portal**: 
   1. Use your username and password to access your account.
2. **Navigate to Security Settings**: 
   1. Find the section labeled **Security** or **Account Settings**.
   2. Look for an option that says **Two-Factor Authentication** or **Enable 2FA**.
3. **Select Google Authenticator**:
   1. Choose the option to set up Google Authenticator as your 2FA method.

## Step 3: Set Up Google Authenticator

1. **Choose Setup Method**:
   1. You will typically be presented with a QR code or a setup key. QR codes are easier to use.
2. **Open Google Authenticator**:
   1. Tap the plus sign (+) in the bottom right corner of the app.
   2. Select Scan a QR code if using a QR code or Enter a setup key if provided with a text code.
3. Scan QR Code or Enter Key:

   1. If scanning, point your camera at the QR code displayed on your Partner Portal.

      <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/cce7d0b092e62a35e1cee605335b4db428ab6bdc0a6d1906b142fc1103375973-image-20241015-050006.png" />
   2. If entering manually, type in the account name and setup key as instructed.

## Step 4: Verify Setup

1. Receive Verification Code:

   1. After successfully adding your account, Google Authenticator will generate a six-digit code.

      <Image align="center" className="border" border={true} src="https://files.readme.io/42df5d86fc91da32f377907392690ccbd2d2d9066d5015c3633a9f91e116628e-image-11.png" />
2. Enter Code in Partner Portal:
   1. Return to the Partner Portal and enter the verification code in the designated field.
   2. Confirm or save your settings.

## Step 5: Using Google Authenticator for Future Logins

1. Log into Partner Portal:
   1. Enter your username and password as usual.
2. Input Verification Code:
   1. Open Google Authenticator, find the code associated with your Partner Portal account and enter it when prompted.
   2. Remember that codes refresh every 30 seconds, so ensure you enter them promptly.

This guide should help you effectively set up and use Google Authenticator for enhanced security on your Partner Portal. For any issues during setup, refer to your organization's IT support or help desk.
